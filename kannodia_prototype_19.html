PB-2026-0019 — what went wrong

Kept here as the reference case for the fix above.

JOB-2026-0019 targeted ktc bs 2side, a Bedsheet whose routing is Cutting → Stitching → Ironing. The tracking log shows it actually ran through:

dohar fabric jointfalalain jointdohar final stitchpillow flatlock

All four belong to other products' routings. Because batch creation didn't require a Pattern, the job wasn't filtered to any one routing at all.
